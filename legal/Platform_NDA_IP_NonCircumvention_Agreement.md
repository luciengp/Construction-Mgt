**AGREEMENT**

This Platform Access, Confidentiality, Intellectual Property and
Non-Circumvention Agreement (the **\"Agreement\"**) is a binding
contract between the Provider and the User. By clicking \"I agree\",
creating an account, or accessing or using the Platform, the User
accepts this Agreement and confirms they have the authority to bind each
of their Affiliates to it. If the User does not agree, they must not
access or use the Platform.

**1. Definitions and interpretation**

**1.1** In this Agreement, the following terms have the following
meanings:

> \(a\) \"Platform\" means the Provider\'s construction management
> software application and service, in any form (web, mobile,
> application programming interface or otherwise), together with its
> inspection and test plans, checklists, quality-gate logic, dashboards,
> data models, workflows, screen designs, documentation and all related
> materials.
>
> \(b\) \"Affiliate\" means, in relation to any person, (i) any company,
> partnership, joint venture or other entity that such person directly
> or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control
> with; (ii) any entity in which such person holds a material interest
> or of which they are a director, officer, partner, employee,
> consultant or agent; and (iii) any spouse, family member, nominee or
> person acting on their behalf. \"Control\" means the power to direct
> the management or policies of an entity, whether through ownership of
> voting rights, contract or otherwise.
>
> \(c\) \"Confidential Information\" means all non-public information
> disclosed by or on behalf of the Provider or otherwise obtained
> through the Platform, whether or not marked confidential, including
> the items listed in Clause 3.1, and any analyses or derivatives of it.
>
> \(d\) \"Intellectual Property Rights\" means all patents, copyrights,
> database rights, design rights, trade marks, trade secrets, know-how
> and all other intellectual property rights, whether registered or
> unregistered, anywhere in the world.
>
> \(e\) \"Competing Product\" means any software, application, service
> or system that performs materially the same function as the Platform,
> namely the management of construction inspections, quality gates or
> milestone-based payment control, or that is derived from or
> substantially similar to the Platform.
>
> \(f\) \"Feedback\" means any suggestion, idea, enhancement request or
> other input the User provides about the Platform.

**1.2** References to the User include, and bind, each of the User\'s
Affiliates. The User procures that each of its Affiliates complies with
this Agreement as if that Affiliate were the User, and the User is
liable for any act or omission of its Affiliates that would breach this
Agreement if done by the User.

**1.3** Headings are for convenience only. \"Including\" means
\"including without limitation\".

**2. Access and permitted use**

**2.1** Subject to this Agreement, the Provider grants the User a
limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable licence to access
and use the Platform solely for the User\'s internal purpose of managing
their own construction project(s).

**2.2** The User must not, and must ensure its Affiliates do not:

> \(a\) copy, reproduce, republish, distribute, sell, sub-licence, rent
> or lend the Platform or any part of it;
>
> \(b\) reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble or otherwise attempt to
> derive the source code, structure, logic or underlying ideas of the
> Platform, except to the minimum extent this restriction is prohibited
> by applicable law;
>
> \(c\) access the Platform to build, or assist any person to build, a
> Competing Product, or to monitor its availability, performance or
> functionality for any competitive purpose;
>
> \(d\) remove or obscure any proprietary notice; or
>
> \(e\) use the Platform other than as expressly permitted by this
> Agreement.

**3. Confidentiality**

**3.1** The User acknowledges that the Platform embodies valuable
Confidential Information of the Provider, including: the master
inspection and test plan and its structure; the checklists and
acceptance criteria; the quality-gate and milestone-payment logic; the
data model, workflows and business rules; the screen designs and user
experience; pricing; and any non-public information about the
Provider\'s business, customers or technology.

**3.2** The User must, and must ensure its Affiliates: (a) keep the
Confidential Information strictly confidential; (b) use it only to use
the Platform as permitted by this Agreement; (c) not disclose it to any
third party without the Provider\'s prior written consent; and (d)
protect it with at least the same degree of care they use for their own
confidential information, and in no event less than a reasonable degree
of care.

**3.3** The obligations in Clause 3.2 do not apply to information that
the User can show, by written evidence, is or becomes public through no
breach of this Agreement, was lawfully known to the User before
disclosure, or is lawfully received from a third party without
restriction. Where disclosure is required by law or court order, the
User must, where lawful, give the Provider prompt notice and reasonable
assistance to seek protective treatment.

**3.4** The confidentiality obligations survive termination and continue
for so long as the information remains confidential, and in respect of
trade secrets, for as long as they remain trade secrets under applicable
law.

**4. Intellectual property**

**4.1** As between the parties, the Provider owns and retains all
Intellectual Property Rights in and to the Platform and all Confidential
Information. Nothing in this Agreement transfers any ownership to the
User. All rights not expressly granted are reserved.

**4.2** The User assigns to the Provider, with full title guarantee and
by way of present and future assignment, all Intellectual Property
Rights it may have in any modification, derivative or improvement of the
Platform that it creates in breach of this Agreement, and will do all
things reasonably necessary to give effect to this assignment.

**4.3** The User grants the Provider a perpetual, irrevocable,
worldwide, royalty-free licence to use, incorporate and exploit any
Feedback for any purpose, without obligation or attribution.

**4.4** The User retains ownership of the project data it enters (such
as its own inspection records and photographs), and grants the Provider
a licence to host and process that data to provide the Platform. This
Clause 4.4 does not give the User any rights in the Platform itself.

**5. Data protection**

**5.1** Each party will comply with applicable data-protection law in
respect of personal data processed through the Platform. The Provider
will process personal data in accordance with its privacy policy, as
updated from time to time.

**6. Non-competition**

**6.1** The User must not, and must ensure its Affiliates do not, during
the period the User has access to the Platform and for **\[twelve (12)
months\]** after that access ends, directly or indirectly design,
develop, build, launch, fund, own, operate or assist any person to
create a Competing Product that is derived from, or that uses,
incorporates or is substantially based on, the Provider\'s Confidential
Information.

**6.2** Clause 6.1 does not prevent the User from independently
developing a product without any use of or reference to the Provider\'s
Confidential Information, provided the User can demonstrate such
independent development by contemporaneous written records.

**6.3** The parties consider the restraint in this Clause 6 to be
reasonable and necessary to protect the Provider\'s legitimate interests
in its Confidential Information and Intellectual Property. If any part
is found to be unenforceable, Clause 11.5 (severance and reading down)
applies.

**7. Non-circumvention**

**7.1** The User must not, and must ensure its Affiliates do not, use
the Platform, the Confidential Information, or any relationship or
introduction arising through the Platform to circumvent the Provider or
deprive it of the benefit of its work, including by:

> \(a\) engaging, soliciting or contracting directly with any developer,
> contractor, supplier, customer or partner first identified or
> introduced through the Platform, in a manner that bypasses the
> Provider, for the purpose of replicating or replacing the Platform;
>
> \(b\) replicating the Platform\'s inspection plans, checklists or
> quality-gate logic for use outside the Provider\'s service; or
>
> \(c\) procuring or encouraging any third party to do anything the User
> is prohibited from doing under this Agreement.

**7.2** This Clause 7 applies during the User\'s access to the Platform
and for \[twelve (12) months\] afterwards.

**8. User warranties**

**8.1** The User warrants that: (a) it has full authority to enter into
this Agreement and to bind each of its Affiliates; (b) the information
it provides at registration is accurate; and (c) its use of the Platform
will comply with this Agreement and applicable law.

**9. Remedies and enforcement**

**9.1** The User acknowledges that a breach of Clauses 2, 3, 4, 6 or 7
may cause the Provider irreparable harm for which damages alone would be
an inadequate remedy, and that the Provider is entitled to seek
injunctive or other equitable relief, in addition to any other remedy,
without needing to prove actual damage or post security.

**9.2** The User will indemnify the Provider against all losses,
damages, costs and expenses (including reasonable legal fees) arising
from the User\'s or its Affiliates\' breach of this Agreement.

**9.3** The Provider may suspend or terminate the User\'s access
immediately on any breach, without liability.

**10. Term and termination**

**10.1** This Agreement begins when the User first accepts it and
continues until the User\'s account is closed. Clauses 3, 4, 6, 7, 9, 11
and 12, and any provision that by its nature should survive, survive
termination.

**11. General**

**11.1** Entire agreement. This Agreement, with any order form and
privacy policy, is the entire agreement between the parties on its
subject matter and supersedes all prior understandings.

**11.2** Variation. The Provider may update this Agreement by posting a
new version and requiring acceptance; continued use after the effective
date constitutes acceptance.

**11.3** Assignment. The User may not assign this Agreement without the
Provider\'s consent. The Provider may assign it to a successor or
affiliate.

**11.4** No waiver. A failure to enforce any right is not a waiver of
it.

**11.5** Severance and reading down. If any provision (or part) is found
to be invalid or unenforceable, it will be read down to the minimum
extent necessary to be enforceable, or if that is not possible, severed,
and the rest of the Agreement continues in force. In particular, if any
restraint period, scope or geographic area in Clause 6 or 7 is found
unreasonable, it applies as if reduced to the maximum that would be
enforceable.

**11.6** Governing law and jurisdiction. This Agreement is governed by
the laws of **\[ Thailand \]**. The parties submit to the **\[
non-exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Thailand / arbitration under
the Thai Arbitration Institute \]**, subject to the Provider\'s right to
seek injunctive relief in any competent court.

**11.7** Notices. Notices to the User may be given through the Platform
or to the email on the account; notices to the Provider must be sent to
\[ Provider notice address / email \].

**12. Electronic acceptance and record**

**12.1** The User accepts this Agreement electronically by ticking the
acceptance box and clicking \"I agree\" at registration. The parties
agree that this constitutes a valid, binding signature.

**12.2** The Provider will record, and the User consents to the
recording of: the User\'s name and account email; the identity of the
Affiliate(s) the User declares they represent; the version of this
Agreement accepted; and the date, time and IP address of acceptance.
That record is evidence of the User\'s acceptance and the terms
accepted.

**12.3** Affiliate declaration. At registration the User will be asked
to confirm: \"I accept these terms for myself and for every company or
entity I represent or am connected with (my Affiliates), and I have
authority to bind them.\" A record of that confirmation is kept.
